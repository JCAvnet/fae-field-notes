About FAE Field Notes_

A personal project by the ANZ Avnet AMD FAE team: field notes, in our own words.

What it is

We are the field applications engineers (FAEs) at Avnet in Australia and New Zealand who work with AMD technology. FAE Field Notes is a side project we run ourselves: each week one of us writes up a real project in the first person, whether a demo, a customer proof of concept or a lab experiment. We say what we built, how it works, what went wrong, what we learned and why it might matter to you. It is written in plain English for engineers, managers and customers alike, and every number is backed by a source.

FAE Field Notes is an independent, personal project by the ANZ Avnet AMD FAE team. It is not an official AMD or Avnet publication, and the views are the authors' own. AMD and Avnet are trademarks of their respective owners.

Each issue is published here, sent as an email and shared on LinkedIn. You can also follow along with the RSS feed.

How each issue is made

  1. An FAE shares their notes. Contributors are FAEs invited by the editor. They supply their notes, key numbers and where they come from, plus photos or screenshots.
  2. We draft it. An AI assistant turns the notes into an article in the author's own voice, an email and a LinkedIn post, and runs the quality checks (structure, first-person voice, evidence for every number, image sizes).
  3. We review, then publish. The author and the editor review the draft until it is right. Once the editor approves it, it goes live here.
